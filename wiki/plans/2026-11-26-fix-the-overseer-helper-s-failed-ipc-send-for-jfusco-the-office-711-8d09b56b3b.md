# Fix the Overseer helper’s failed IPC send for JFusco/the-office#711

## Problem and evidence

- Phase 7c reported one failure on `codex/702-triage-corroboration` from `32e21c8` at about load 9. The broad run passed 381 of 382 files. Two subsequent isolated runs each passed 16 of 16. [Phase 7c record in issue #711](https://github.com/JFusco/the-office/issues/711)
- Quality run `45910947712`, attempt 1, macOS job `140218946844` failed at `launcher-process.test.ts:309:84` on PR #707 head `4f596e75`. [Attempt 1 job](https://github.com/JFusco/the-office/actions/runs/45910947712/job/140218946844)
- Attempt 2, macOS job `140223458453`, failed at the same assertion on the same head. [Attempt 2 job](https://github.com/JFusco/the-office/actions/runs/45910947712/job/140223458453) · [Rerun summary](https://github.com/JFusco/the-office/actions/runs/45910947712/attempts/2)

The hosted annotations establish that the 25-second check for both PIDs to disappear returned false. The surviving PID and helper exit reason are unknown. Both jobs exercised the same timing window. The test kills the launcher one second after requesting TERM, while the helper drains inherited pipes for up to one second after the pass exits. Those waits start at different events and can overlap on successive runs.

On Quality’s Node 24.14.0, an IPC write that returns an error can queue an `error` event on the sending process when the caller omits the callback. The helper’s synchronous catch cannot intercept that event. Its `child.on('error')` listener observes the pass. A fatal send error can therefore end the helper before disconnect cleanup escalates to KILL. The draft author reported two uncaught EPIPE exits in 20 large-message probe runs and zero in 60 callback runs, with eight callback errors. The probe used 256 KiB messages. Its transcript and the full historical job logs were unavailable. Record the launcher defect and qualify its role in the three historical failures as the supported hypothesis. [Node 24.14.0 IPC implementation](https://github.com/nodejs/node/blob/v24.14.0/lib/internal/child_process.js#L689-L829) · [Node 24.14.0 error-event behavior](https://nodejs.org/download/release/v24.14.0/docs/api/events.html#error-events)
