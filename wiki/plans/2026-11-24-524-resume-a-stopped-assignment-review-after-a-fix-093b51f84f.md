# #524 — Resume a stopped assignment review after a fix

## Revised acceptance criterion

Joe chose **“Resume it once.”** An otherwise eligible stopped review gets one `review-assignment` admission per incident, bound intake subject and fix event. This includes `retry.exhausted === true`, `retry.attempts >= 3` with the flag still false, `itRecoveryAttempts >= 3`, and both budgets spent together. The saved counters and complete `retry` value must equal their pre-admission values, including the absence of optional fields. A repeat returns the original completed receipt. This criterion replaces the saved issue’s spent-budget refusal.
