# Feature planning and engineering lessons

Feature Discovery produces a reviewable brief. Feature Planner turns an approved brief into implementation-ready tickets with acceptance and verification requirements. The original two-stage model landed in PR #39.

Harper's knowledge loop began as a broader continuous-learning plan. Later deliveries reduced it to demand-driven lessons, retained evidence, and explicit curation outcomes. Use the knowledge-loop document for the current contract and the archived plan for the historical proposal.
